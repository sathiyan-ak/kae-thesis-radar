# Submission positioning

## One-line description

I converted Kae Capital’s latest public healthtech opportunity map into a live startup-sourcing and first-pass diligence system.

## Why it is relevant to the internship

The tool demonstrates the repeated work behind an investment memo: structuring a market, identifying companies, checking founder and business-model fit, separating evidence from assumptions, articulating a bear case and preparing the questions required for an investment-team meeting.

## Honest scope

This is a 1–2 day MVP and work sample. It does not claim to reproduce Kae’s internal thesis, pipeline or investment process. The seed company scores are analyst hypotheses that must be refreshed with current primary-source and private-company evidence.
